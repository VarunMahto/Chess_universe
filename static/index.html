♟ CHESS HELP

Piece movement, special rules and controls.

♙ PawnMoves forward, two squares on its first move, captures diagonally and can promote.
♘ KnightMoves in an L shape and jumps over pieces.
♗ BishopMoves diagonally through clear squares.
♖ RookMoves horizontally or vertically.
♕ QueenMoves any distance horizontally, vertically or diagonally.
♔ KingMoves one square and may castle under the special rules.

Special rules

Castling · En passant · Promotion · Check · Checkmate · Stalemate · Threefold repetition · 50-move rule · Insufficient material.

Controls

Click or drag a piece. Click another same-colour piece to change selection. Legal destinations glow.
